Privacy Policy

Musikli · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Summary

Musikli is a music puzzle game. You can play the whole game as a guest. We collect as little as possible, we do not sell your data, and you can delete everything at any time from inside the app.

What we collect

Player name and avatar you choose. They are shown to other players on the weekly leaderboard, so do not use your real name if you prefer to stay anonymous.

E-mail address and password, only if you choose to create an account. The password is stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see it.

Game progress: unlocked levels, stars, scores, coins, items and settings. This is stored on your device and, when online features are available, on our server so it can be restored on another device.

Weekly score, used to build the leaderboard.

A random account identifier created for you (including for guests) so your leaderboard entry and saved progress belong to you.

What we do not collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or files. The game does not request any of these permissions.

No advertising identifier and no third-party analytics in this version.

No payment information. This version has no purchases with real money.

How we use it

To run the game: save and restore your progress, show the weekly leaderboard, and let you sign in.

To keep the leaderboard fair, for example by rejecting impossible scores and offensive names.

We do not use your data for advertising profiles and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Who processes it

Online data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication hosting), acting as our service provider. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

If a future version adds advertising or purchases, this policy and the Google Play data safety section will be updated before that version is released.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Weekly scores are kept as leaderboard history until you delete your account.

Delete from the app: Settings > Delete account. This permanently removes your account, profile, saved progress and all leaderboard entries from our server, and clears the game data on your device.

Delete without the app: e-mail lvlupkli@gmail.com from the address of your account and ask for deletion. We complete requests within 30 days.

Guests who never went online have no data on our server; uninstalling the app or using Settings > Reset removes everything.

Children

Musikli is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to its processing. You can do most of this directly in the app (edit profile, delete account); for anything else, contact us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in an important way we will update the date above and show a notice in the app.

Questions or requests: lvlupkli@gmail.com
