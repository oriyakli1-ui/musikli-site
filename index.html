Musikli

Free the notes. Play the melody.

Musikli is a free music puzzle game for Android where you tap winding notes off a packed board, and every note you free plays the next part of a famous public-domain melody. When the board is clear you hear the whole tune.

What is in the game

  • 50 levels across 5 worlds, each built on a different well-known melody.
  • Nursery tunes, folk songs, carols and classical themes by Beethoven, Mozart, Bach, Vivaldi, Tchaikovsky and more.
  • A new daily challenge every day and a weekly champions leaderboard.
  • Five instruments to unlock, from grand piano to retro arcade chip.
  • Free to play. No account needed.

Frequently asked questions

How do you play Musikli?
Tap a note to send it straight ahead off the board. If another note or a rest-stone is in its way it bumps back and you lose a heart. Clear every note before you run out of hearts or time.

Is the music in Musikli copyrighted?
No. Every melody is in the public domain: traditional tunes and works by composers who died more than 100 years ago. All sounds are generated by the game itself.

Does Musikli work offline?
Yes. All 50 levels and the daily challenge work without a connection. Only accounts and the global leaderboard need the internet.

Is Musikli free?
Yes. The game is free to play and can be played as a guest without creating an account.

Which melodies are in Musikli?
50 well-known tunes, including Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star, Ode to Joy, Fur Elise, Eine kleine Nachtmusik, Bridal Chorus, Swan Lake, Flight of the Bumblebee.

How do I delete my Musikli account?
In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. You can also request deletion by e-mail.
