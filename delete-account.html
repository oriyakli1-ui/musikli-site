Delete your Musikli account

This page explains how to delete your Musikli account and the data linked to it. Developer: Musikli Games.

Option 1: inside the app (instant)

  1. Open Musikli.
  2. Tap your player name at the top of the home screen to open Settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Option 2: by e-mail (if you no longer have the app)

Send an e-mail to lvlupkli@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Musikli account". We complete requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (e-mail address, hashed password).
  • Your player name and avatar.
  • Your saved game progress (levels, stars, coins, items).
  • All of your weekly leaderboard scores.

What is kept

Nothing. No data linked to your account is retained after deletion. Guests who never went online have no data on our servers; uninstalling the app removes everything.
